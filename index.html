Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Work Hours works straight away without any sign-up. Your hours are stored on your device and synced through your own iCloud account.

Where is my data stored?

Your jobs, shifts, breaks and notes are saved on your device and in your personal iCloud, so they stay in sync between your iPhone and iPad. We don't keep a copy of your timesheets on our servers.

How is overtime calculated?

For each job you choose how many hours a day are paid at your normal rate. Any time worked beyond that is paid at the overtime rate you set, and it's shown separately in your timesheet and reports.

Can I track more than one job?

Yes. Add as many jobs or projects as you like, each with its own hourly rate, currency and overtime rules, and switch between them in one tap.

I forgot to check in. Can I add a shift later?

Of course. Open any day to add a shift or break, or tap an existing time to correct it. Your hours and pay are recalculated instantly.

What formats can I export?

You can export timesheets as PDF, Excel (XLSX) or CSV, and choose whether to include breaks, notes and individual shift rows.

How do I get help?

Email us at support@replykitapp.com or use Contact Us in the app's Settings. We're happy to help.
