1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Work Hours – Paid Time Tracker app ("Work Hours", "the app", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad and when you visit this website. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data you enter in the app

To track your time, you can enter information such as:

  • Job or project names, hourly rates, overtime rules and currency
  • Check-in and check-out times, shifts and breaks
  • Notes you add to a day
  • Preferences such as language, theme, week start day and reminder times

This information is stored locally on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, in your private iCloud account. It is not sent to our servers, and we do not have access to it. Timesheets you export (PDF, Excel or CSV) are created on your device and are only shared where you choose to send them.

3. iCloud sync

Work Hours uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to keep your data in sync across your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored in your private iCloud database and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can turn off iCloud for Work Hours at any time in your device's Settings; your data will then stay on your device only.

4. Usage information we collect

When you use the app, we and our analytics service providers automatically collect certain information about how the app is used. This may include:

  • App activity: features you use and actions you take in the app (for example, checking in, adding a break, viewing a report or exporting a timesheet), screens you visit and how you interact with them, and settings chosen such as currency or whether overtime is enabled.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, device and app language, and general region derived from your IP address.
  • Identifiers: a randomly generated app or installation identifier and the date the app was first opened, used to count active users and sessions.
  • Diagnostics: information about app performance, errors and crashes.

We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the app, and we do not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

5. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the app and its features
  • To understand which features are used and where people run into difficulty
  • To diagnose crashes, bugs and performance issues
  • To update certain app settings remotely (such as support contact details) without requiring an app update
  • To reply to your support requests
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the app, on your consent where required, and on legal obligations.

6. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share usage information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the app and who process it on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config) — app usage analytics and remote configuration. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Product analytics providers — to help us understand how the app is used and improve the experience.
  • Apple — iCloud sync, notifications and App Store distribution.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

7. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders to check in and check out are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in your device's Settings.

8. Data retention

Data you enter stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it or delete the app. Usage information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above, after which it is deleted or anonymised, in line with our service providers' retention settings.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email us at support@replykitapp.com. You can also:

  • Delete your entries in the app, or delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage)
  • Turn off notifications or iCloud sync for the app in your device's Settings

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. Your time-tracking data is protected by your device's security and by Apple's iCloud protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

Work Hours is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we may also notify you in the app. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

14. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at support@replykitapp.com.
